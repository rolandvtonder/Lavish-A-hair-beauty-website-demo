Before you book

Common questions

How do I book an appointment?

+

Send us a WhatsApp or call 067 647 8964. Tell us the service you want and when suits you, and we will confirm a time. You can also use the booking form on our Contact page.

Where is LAVISH A?

+

We are at 56 Voortrekker Road, Goodwood, Cape Town. Look for the gold LAVISH A sign above the window. We are easy to reach from Parow, Vasco, Thornton, Epping and Elsies River.

Do you work with natural, curly and coily hair?

+

Yes. We work with every hair type and texture: straight, wavy, curly, coily and natural. That includes silk presses, natural blow-outs, two-strand twists, comb coils, cuts and fades.

Do you do nails and pedicures too?

+

Yes. We do manicures, acrylic and gel nails, nail art, French tips, soak-offs and pedicures in our recliner chairs with foot spas, so you can do hair and nails in one visit.

How much do your services cost?

+

Prices depend on your hair length, thickness and the look you want. We confirm your price at consultation before we start, and you are welcome to ask for a quote on WhatsApp first.

Can I buy hair products from you?

+

Yes. We sell shampoo & conditioner sets in eight flavours for R120 a set, and Veaudry myDryer Compact and Pro hair dryers. Order on WhatsApp at 074 768 2815. Laybuy is available on the myDryer Pro.

Do you offer training for stylists?

+

Yes. We host hands-on in-salon training, such as Japanese Silk hair treatment techniques and scalp care sessions. Spots are limited, so WhatsApp 074 768 2815 to ask about the next date.
